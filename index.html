What Makes This Movie Iconic

This movie combined a universe of characters, drawn and popularized previously in comics and animations for years, and incorporated them into the Marvel Cinematic Universe, one of the largest and most successful film franchieses ever created. Going to the theaters each year to see a Marvel movie was one of the happiest parts of my childhood, and they inspired me to pursue STEM. This movie is the one that united the Marvel Cinematic Universe.
